How It Works

Step-by-step guide to maximizing your earnings

Getting Started

  • Connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.)
  • Ensure you have USDT (BEP-20) on BNB Smart Chain
  • Obtain a referrer link or use the default platform referrer

Making Your First Investment

Minimum investment is 1 USDT. A 10% management fee is deducted from your deposit, with the remaining 90% deposited into Venus Protocol to generate yield.

Example: Invest 100 USDT → 10 USDT fee → 90 USDT to Venus → Earn 0.3% daily on 90 USDT

Earning Daily ROI

Base daily ROI is 0.3% (30 basis points). This is calculated based on your active deposit and accumulates every second. You can claim your ROI anytime with minimum 1 USDT.

Referral Program

Earn commissions from your team's investments across 5 levels. Qualification requirements apply for each level to ensure network quality.

Level 1: 5% (Min 10 USDT)
Level 2: 3% (Min 30 USDT + 3 directs)
Level 3: 2% (Min 50 USDT + 5 directs)
Level 4: 1% (Min 70 USDT + 8 directs)
Level 5: 0.5% (Min 100 USDT + 10 directs)

Rank System

Advance through 5 ranks to unlock ROI boosts. Higher ranks require team volume, qualified directs, and minimum personal deposits.

Withdrawal Process

  • ROI Withdrawal: Claim anytime, minimum 1 USDT
  • Capital Withdrawal: Available after 60 days lock period
  • Early Withdrawal: 50% fee applies if withdrawn before lock period ends

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What is NanoTrust?
NanoTrust is a decentralized yield infrastructure that connects investors with Venus Protocol lending markets. It offers sustainable daily ROI through professional yield optimization and a multi-level referral system.
Is the smart contract audited?
Yes, the NanoTrust smart contract has been audited by reputable security firms. The contract is also verified on BSCScan and uses OpenZeppelin's battle-tested libraries for security.
How is the daily ROI generated?
Your deposits are automatically deposited into Venus Protocol, a leading DeFi lending platform on BNB Smart Chain. The yields generated from lending markets are distributed back to investors as daily ROI.
What happens if I withdraw early?
Early withdrawal (before 60 days) incurs a 50% fee on your principal amount. This fee is designed to maintain platform stability and prevent manipulation. After 60 days, you can withdraw your full principal without any fees.
How do I qualify for referral commissions?
Each referral level has specific requirements: Level 1 requires 10 USDT minimum deposit, Level 2 requires 30 USDT + 3 qualified directs, Level 3 requires 50 USDT + 5 qualified directs, Level 4 requires 70 USDT + 8 qualified directs, and Level 5 requires 100 USDT + 10 qualified directs.
What is a "qualified direct"?
A qualified direct is a referral who has made an active deposit of at least 50 USDT. This ensures that your team consists of serious investors and helps maintain the sustainability of the referral system.
