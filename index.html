FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NanoTrust and how does it work?
NanoTrust is a decentralized yield infrastructure powered by Venus Protocol. Users deposit USDT which is then supplied to Venus Protocol to generate yields. Investors earn 0.3% base daily ROI, with additional boosts based on their leadership rank (up to +100% for Diamond rank = 0.6% daily).
What is the minimum investment amount?
The minimum investment amount is 1 USDT. A 10% management fee is deducted from each deposit, with the remaining 90% deposited to Venus Protocol.
How does the 60-day lock period work?
All investments have a 60-day lock period. During this period, you can claim daily ROI. Early withdrawal before 60 days incurs a 50% fee. After 60 days, you can withdraw without fees.
How do leadership ranks work?
There are 5 ranks: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond. Each rank provides ROI boosts from 0.1% to 1%. Requirements include team volume, qualified directs, and personal deposit.
