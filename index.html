How It Works

Learn how to maximize your earnings

Registration

To join NanoForce, you need to pay a registration fee of 4 USDT. This automatically activates Level 1 in both X3 and X6 matrices. You also receive 1 Lucky Draw ticket upon registration.

Early Bird Bonus: First 7 days after launch enjoy 10% discount on all level purchases!

Matrix X3 System

The X3 matrix has 3 positions below you. When all 3 positions are filled:

  • First 2 partners pay directly to you
  • 3rd partner payment auto-reinvests the level for you
  • Matrix resets and cycle repeats
  • If you don't upgrade, payments go to your upline

Matrix X6 System

The X6 matrix has 2 branches:

  • First Branch: 2 positions (payments go to your referrer)
  • Second Branch: 4 positions (3 pay to you, 4th auto-reinvests)
  • More spillover potential than X3

Dividend System

40% of every matrix payment is distributed as dividends to all qualified users. To qualify for dividends, you must have purchased at least Level 2 in either matrix. Free users can accumulate locked points until they upgrade.

Staking

Stake USDT to earn 0.3% daily ROI. Maximum profit is 150% of your stake amount. You can claim rewards daily or unstake anytime to receive your principal back. 10% of staking pool contributes to Lucky Draw jackpot.

Lucky Draw

Weekly draws every 7 days. Jackpot consists of draw pool + 10% of staking pool. Each ticket gives you one entry. Winners selected randomly. Maximum 1000 participants per draw. Get tickets by participating (free), buying levels, or staking.
