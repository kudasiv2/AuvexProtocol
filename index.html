FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NanoTrust and how does it work?
NanoTrust is a decentralized yield infrastructure powered by Venus Protocol. Users deposit USDT which is then supplied to Venus Protocol to generate yields. Investors earn 0.3% base daily ROI, with additional boosts based on their leadership rank. The platform operates fully on-chain with transparent smart contracts.
What is the minimum investment amount?
The minimum investment amount is 1 USDT. There is no maximum limit. A 10% management fee is deducted from each deposit, with the remaining 90% being deposited to Venus Protocol to start generating yields immediately.
How does the 60-day lock period work?
All investments have a 60-day lock period. During this period, you can still claim your daily ROI earnings. However, if you choose to withdraw your capital before the 60 days complete, a 50% early withdrawal fee will apply. After 60 days, you can withdraw your capital without any fees.
How do leadership ranks and ROI boosts work?
There are 5 leadership ranks (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) that provide ROI boosts from 10% to 100%. To achieve ranks, you need team volume, qualified directs (referrals with 50+ USDT active deposit), and minimum personal deposits. For example, Diamond rank requires 100,000 USDT team volume, 1000 qualified directs, and 100 USDT personal deposit, giving you 100% ROI boost (0.6% daily instead of 0.3%).
What are the referral commission rates?
NanoTrust offers a 6-level referral system with commissions: Level 1: 5%, Level 2: 3%, Level 3: 2%, Level 4: 1%, Level 5: 0.5%, Level 6: 0.5%. To unlock higher levels, you need to meet qualification criteria including minimum deposits and number of qualified directs. Commissions are paid instantly to your pending referral balance.
How do I qualify for referral commissions?
To earn commissions from each level, you must meet qualification requirements: Level 1 requires 10 USDT deposit, Level 2 requires 30 USDT + 3 qualified directs, Level 3 requires 50 USDT + 5 directs, Level 4 requires 70 USDT + 8 directs, and Level 5 requires 100 USDT + 10 directs. A qualified direct is a referral with at least 50 USDT active deposit.
Is the smart contract audited and secure?
Yes, NanoTrust uses OpenZeppelin's battle-tested contracts including ReentrancyGuard and SafeERC20. The contract is verified on BSCScan and uses industry-standard security practices. However, as with any DeFi protocol, users should understand the risks involved and only invest what they can afford to lose. The contract address is: 0x0d00583f6E99e1370e2DB18a7239C2a18eb7065F
